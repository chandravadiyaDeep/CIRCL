A few clear terms for using CIRCL.

These draft terms cover the CIRCL website. Creator participation is also governed by the separately accepted Creator Agreement.

Website use

Use this website lawfully and respectfully. Do not attempt to disrupt the website, probe systems without authorization, submit malicious content or misrepresent your identity.

No growth guarantee

CIRCL provides a creator network and opportunities for organic discovery and collaboration. It does not promise any number of followers, views, reach, engagement, channel joins or other results. Platform distribution and audience behaviour are outside CIRCL's control.

Creator content

Creators retain rights to their own content. Submission of an application or participation in the network does not transfer ownership to CIRCL. Any permission to feature or reuse content should be specific and documented.

Third-party platforms

CIRCL uses Instagram as its initial community platform. Instagram is operated by a third party and its features, eligibility, availability, policies and algorithms may change. Use of Instagram is also subject to Instagram's own terms and policies.

Applications and participation

Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance. The Creator Agreement and Community Guidelines govern accepted creator participation. CIRCL may update application procedures and website content.

Website availability and changes

The website is provided on an as-available basis. CIRCL may update, suspend or remove website features. Nothing in these draft terms excludes rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded.

Contact and governing law

The operator's legal name, contact address and appropriate governing-law and jurisdiction details must be added before public launch. These terms require review for applicable Indian law and should not be treated as final legal advice.
