ABOUT CIRCL

A network built around shared discovery.

CIRCL is an independent creator network connecting creators and audiences through organic discovery, collaboration and shared community.

Our idea

Creators build communities around their own voices. CIRCL creates connections between those communities so people can discover more creators they may genuinely enjoy.

What we believe

We believe growth should be earned through relevant content and real audience interest. We do not sell followers, promise specific results or ask people to follow creators in exchange for access.

Our focus

We are starting with Instagram and lifestyle-led creator categories in India, including fashion, daily lifestyle, beauty, grooming and entertainment. Our first job is to build a healthy creator network and make collaboration easier.

What CIRCL is not

CIRCL is not a follower exchange, engagement pod, paid-growth service or guarantee of reach. It is a structured network for introductions, collaborative content and transparent organic discovery.

CIRCL is focused on organic creator discovery and collaboration. No follower or engagement outcomes are guaranteed.
