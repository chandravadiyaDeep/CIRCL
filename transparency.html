GROWTH & TRANSPARENCY

Clear about the process. Careful with the numbers.

We share what we measure, where the data comes from and where attribution has limits. No inflated claims. No guaranteed growth promises.

What organic growth means

CIRCL creates opportunities for people to discover creators through invitations, creator features, relevant introductions and collaborative Instagram content. A member may choose to follow one creator, several creators or none. That choice is part of genuine discovery.

Metrics we may report

Story reach and link taps from creator-provided Instagram Insights; referral-link clicks recorded by the CIRCL website; Broadcast Channel member-count changes where available; Reels reach and interactions from Instagram Insights; and profile visits or follows only where the platform provides reliable, authorized data.

How referral links work

A creator may receive a unique CIRCL link containing a non-personal referral code. The website can count link visits associated with that code. It may also store an optional campaign identifier. The link can then open the Instagram destination. A click is not proof that the visitor joined a channel or followed a creator.

Attribution limitations

Instagram does not automatically share a complete person-level path from a CIRCL website click to a Broadcast Channel join and then to a creator follow. Channel growth during a campaign is a time-window comparison, not proof that every new member came from a particular creator. We label estimates as estimates.

Campaign reporting

Where practical, a campaign report records its dates, participating creators, link taps, Story reach supplied by the creator, relevant content performance and channel membership change. Reports should note missing data, overlapping campaigns and other factors that may affect results.

Data minimisation

Referral identifiers should not contain personal information. CIRCL does not need Instagram passwords, private-message access or audience member lists to operate its initial network. We do not use hidden fingerprinting or sell audience data.

CIRCL is focused on organic creator discovery and collaboration. No follower or engagement outcomes are guaranteed.
