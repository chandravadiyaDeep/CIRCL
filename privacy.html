PRIVACY POLICY

Your information deserves care.

This draft explains the intended privacy approach for the CIRCL website and creator network. Replace the contact details and confirm all live service providers before public launch.

Information we may collect

If you submit a creator application, CIRCL may collect your name, Instagram handle and profile URL, content category, approximate follower range, audience location, contact email and application answers. Referral links may record a referral code, campaign code, page path and click timestamp. We should avoid collecting sensitive personal information.

How we use information

Application details are used to assess and manage creator participation, communicate with applicants and administer the network. Referral and campaign information is used to understand campaign activity and prepare aggregate or creator-specific reports. Security and operational logs may be used to protect the website.

What we do not request

CIRCL does not need your Instagram password, authentication code, private messages or a list of your followers. Do not send these details to us.

Sharing and disclosure

CIRCL should not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to host the website, receive applications or measure website activity, with appropriate safeguards. We may disclose information where required by law or to address security and rights concerns.

Retention and deletion

Keep application and campaign information only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose, legitimate operational needs and applicable legal requirements. A contact process should be provided for access, correction, withdrawal or deletion requests, subject to lawful exceptions.

Security and children's privacy

CIRCL should use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards and limit access to authorized people. The network is intended for creators able to enter a valid participation agreement. Do not submit an application on behalf of a child without appropriate authority and safeguards.

Cookies and analytics

The initial referral script uses session storage where available and does not create tracking cookies or fingerprint visitors. If analytics, cookies, embeds or third-party forms are added, this policy and any required consent controls must be updated before use.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@circl.network only after this address is configured and monitored. This draft must be reviewed and updated with the real operator identity, contact details, tools, retention periods and applicable legal requirements before launch.

CIRCL is focused on organic creator discovery and collaboration. No follower or engagement outcomes are guaranteed.
