HOW IT WORKS

From one creator to a connected network.

A simple, Instagram-first process that brings creators together and gives audiences more ways to discover content.

01 — Creators join

Creators apply to participate. CIRCL reviews fit, content quality, audience relevance and willingness to collaborate. Acceptance is not automatic.

02 — Creators invite their audiences

Participating creators can share the official CIRCL Instagram Broadcast Channel invitation through an Instagram Story, profile link or other agreed placement. Campaign links may identify the creator source.

03 — Audiences discover creators

CIRCL introduces participating creators and shares relevant updates. Members can explore profiles and choose who they want to follow. Following every creator is never required.

04 — Creators collaborate

Creators can propose joint Reels, Collab posts, introductions and other agreed content. Each creator approves their own participation and content before publication.

05 — Review the activity

CIRCL reviews available Story insights, referral clicks, channel changes and content performance. Reports distinguish verified platform metrics from estimates and context.

CIRCL is focused on organic creator discovery and collaboration. No follower or engagement outcomes are guaranteed.
