CREATOR GUIDELINES

A shared network needs shared standards.

These proposed founding creator rules explain how CIRCL participation works. The final agreement is confirmed with each creator during onboarding.

01 — Participation

Creators participate voluntarily. Creators agree to share the initial community invitation and contribute to activities they have accepted. There is no compulsory follow-for-follow. Participation does not guarantee followers, reach or a particular outcome.

02 — Promotion

Each creator receives fair opportunities to be considered for relevant features. Self-promotion must be relevant and approved by CIRCL before it is posted in CIRCL-controlled spaces. Paid advertisements are outside the initial organic-growth scope and require separate approval if introduced later. No spam, fake giveaways or misleading claims.

03 — Community conduct

Creators must treat other creators and members respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, threats, spam and deceptive engagement tactics are not permitted. No creator may falsely claim endorsement or partnership by another member.

04 — Content and ownership

Creators retain ownership of their original content. CIRCL and other creators must obtain permission before downloading, editing, reposting or otherwise reusing creator-owned content, except where a separate licence or applicable law permits it. Tagging a creator is not a substitute for permission.

05 — CIRCL account and channel

CIRCL owns and manages its official Instagram account, website and Broadcast Channel. Channel access and collaborator permissions are controlled by the channel owner and may depend on Instagram features and eligibility. Participation does not grant account ownership or unrestricted posting rights.

06 — Transparency and measurement

CIRCL may record campaign link clicks and use performance information supplied by creators or available through authorized Instagram tools. Reports must distinguish observed metrics from estimates. Creators should not share another creator's non-public campaign data without permission.

07 — Privacy and security

Creators must not share passwords or authentication codes with CIRCL. CIRCL will request only information needed for onboarding and network operations, explain its use in the Privacy Policy and restrict access to authorized administrators.

08 — Leaving the network and enforcement

A creator may request to leave at any time through the CIRCL contact channel. CIRCL may pause or end participation for rule violations, safety concerns, inactivity or misuse of the network. CIRCL will handle removal of its own features and access it controls; it cannot guarantee removal of content independently published by others or control Instagram's systems.

Acceptance and versioning

The creator agreement should identify the version, effective date, creator identity, acceptance date and accepted terms. These website guidelines are an informational draft until the final onboarding agreement is approved and accepted.

CIRCL is focused on organic creator discovery and collaboration. No follower or engagement outcomes are guaranteed.
