WHY JOIN CIRCL

A bigger circle creates new possibilities.

CIRCL helps creators connect beyond their current circle. Participation creates opportunities for discovery — not guaranteed follower counts.

Audience discovery

Meet audiences through relevant creator introductions, shared channel updates and collaborative content. People decide which profiles they want to explore and follow.

Creator relationships

Build connections with creators in complementary categories. Relationships can lead to creative ideas, peer learning and future collaborations.

Collaborative content

Plan Instagram Collab posts and Reels with participating creators. When collaborators accept an Instagram collaboration, content can be distributed across participating profiles according to Instagram's product behaviour.

A clear process

Creators can understand participation expectations, promotion rules, content ownership and the metrics CIRCL reports.

No artificial growth

We do not buy followers, automate engagement, require follow-for-follow or promise a specific growth outcome. Organic discovery depends on content, audience interest and platform distribution.

CIRCL is focused on organic creator discovery and collaboration. No follower or engagement outcomes are guaranteed.
